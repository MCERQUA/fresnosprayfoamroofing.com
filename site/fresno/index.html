SPF roofing, silicone coatings, foam roof repair, and commercial insulation for the Fresno metro — from Blackstone Avenue storefronts to Malaga cold storage, 45 minutes from our Tulare County shop.

Fresno is the largest city in the Central Valley and the seat of Fresno County — a metro of half a million people whose economy still runs on what the surrounding farmland produces. Ag packing and processing plants, cold storage warehouses, distribution hubs strung along Highway 99, retail corridors like Blackstone Avenue, and a downtown office core all share one thing in common: enormous amounts of flat and low-slope roof baking under some of the harshest sun in California. Allstate Spray Foam has been protecting those roofs for more than twenty years. We are a family-owned spray polyurethane foam (SPF) contractor — Fully Licensed · Lic #C-2-1052735, BBB Accredited, and a member of the Spray Polyurethane Foam Alliance — and our crews dispatch to Fresno jobs about 45 minutes north via CA-99 from our Tulare County shop.

Why does Fresno need a roofing company that specializes in foam? Because the local climate is a stress test that generic roofing systems fail early. Summer highs run past 100°F for weeks at a stretch, and a dark flat roof surface can hit 160°F or more — cooking asphalt, shrinking single-ply membranes, and driving cooling bills through the ceiling. Winter brings tule fog that keeps rooftops damp for days, so every seam, fastener, and flashing detail gets soaked and tested. Between the two seasons, relentless UV and airborne ag dust from the surrounding orchards and row crops grind away at whatever surface is up there. Spray foam roofing answers all of it at once: a seamless, self-flashing membrane with roughly R-6.5 of insulation per inch, finished with a UV-stable elastomeric or silicone topcoat that reflects the Valley sun instead of absorbing it.

We serve the whole Fresno metro, not just the city limits. Crews regularly work in Clovis, Sanger, Selma, Fowler, Kerman, and Kingsburg, and everything in between — the packing houses off CA-180 heading toward Sanger, the logistics and industrial parks of south Fresno, Calwa, and Malaga, metal ag buildings out toward Kerman on CA-180 west, and commercial strips along CA-41 and CA-168 through north Fresno and Clovis. If your building sits within sight of Highways 99, 41, 168, or 180, you are inside our normal service pattern. Being 45 minutes away — not four hours — also means the relationship survives the installation: warranty visits, recoat scheduling, and quick post-storm checks are a phone call, not a mobilization negotiation.

Four services cover nearly everything a Fresno building owner needs from us. Spray foam roofing is the flagship: new SPF systems installed over existing flat, low-slope, and metal roofs, typically $4–$8 per square foot installed. Silicone roof coating restores aging foam, metal, and smooth-surface roofs for a fraction of replacement cost — recoats usually run $2–$4 per square foot and reset the clock on a 10–20 year maintenance cycle. Roof repair handles leaks, ponding, blisters, and storm damage on foam and flat roofs; our long-standing Fresno foam roof repair page covers that service in depth, and it remains the fastest way to get a leaking Fresno roof looked at. Commercial spray foam insulation brings the same closed-cell chemistry inside — metal building walls and roof decks, warehouse ceilings, and cold storage envelopes where every degree of thermal control shows up on the utility bill.

Every engagement starts the same way, and it costs you nothing: a free assessment that includes a drone survey of the entire roof field, core sampling where the roof's history is unclear, and a written bid delivered within 48 hours. No pressure, no vague allowances — a documented scope you can hold us to. Because we are the installer and not a broker, the crew that quotes your Fresno roof is the crew network that shows up to do it, backed by industry-leading leak-proof guarantees.

Fresno rewards owners who think about roofs as energy equipment, not just weatherproofing. California's Title 24 energy code already pushes commercial re-roofs toward cool-roof surfaces, and PG&E summer demand charges punish buildings with under-insulated roof decks. An SPF roof addresses the code requirement and the operating cost in a single application — insulation, membrane, and reflective surface in one system. That is why so much of the Valley's food infrastructure, from cold storage docks to packing sheds, has quietly converted to foam over the past two decades.
