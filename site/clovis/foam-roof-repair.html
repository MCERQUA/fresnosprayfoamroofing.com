Flat Roof Repair Across the Fresno-Clovis Metro

Flat and low-slope roofs dominate Clovis's commercial stock, and they fail differently than the pitched shingle roofs most repair advice is written for. On a pitched roof, water runs off; on a flat roof, water lingers, probes, and finds the one detail that summer heat opened up. The repair inventory we see most across the metro: seams and laps split by thermal cycling on built-up and modified bitumen roofs; flashings pulled away from parapets and curbs; punctures and tears from rooftop equipment service; drains and scuppers clogged by fall leaf-drop and harvest dust until water finds another way down; and coatings worn through at walk paths and equipment surrounds. Each of these has a right repair and several wrong ones. Mastic smeared over a wet seam is a wrong one — it holds until the next heat cycle, then fails in the same storm season it was meant to survive. Our repairs cut out the failure, dry the substrate, and rebuild the detail with materials matched to the roof, which is why they hold through the following winter rather than through the following month.

Material matching is the unglamorous half of a repair that lasts. A TPO roof wants heat-welded TPO details, not a smear of universal sealant; a modified bitumen roof wants compatible mod-bit or a properly primed liquid flashing; a coated roof wants the same coating chemistry it already carries, because almost nothing bonds to silicone except silicone. We stock our repair rigs accordingly, and it is a genuine difference between a flat-roof specialist and a general roofer whose truck is set up for shingles. The wrong material on the right spot is still the wrong repair — it just takes one more storm season to prove it.

Foam Roof Repair Done the Way Foam Should Be Repaired

Clovis has a growing population of SPF roofs, and foam repair is its own discipline — one general roofers regularly get wrong. Foam roofs rarely leak in the field; they get damaged at specific points: a dropped tool or hail strike punching the coating, a trade cutting into the roof for a conduit run, birds pecking at an exposed edge, or simply a coating reaching the thin end of its 10-to-20-year cycle. The correct repair is straightforward for a foam contractor: cut out the damaged foam, dry the area, fill with new foam or a compatible sealant, and re-coat with silicone to spec. The incorrect repairs we get called to undo are depressingly consistent — asphalt products smeared onto foam (they attack it), incompatible coatings that never bond, or screwed-down metal patches that add the very penetrations foam exists to eliminate. As the Valley's foam specialists, we repair SPF with SPF-correct methods, and if your foam roof was installed by someone else and never maintained, our foam roof repair service covers the full restoration path from spot repairs to complete recoat.

Finding the Leak: Why Flat Roofs Fool Everyone

The hardest part of flat roof repair is not the fix — it is the find. Water that enters at a parapet crack on the north side can travel laterally along the deck, under insulation, across a beam, and appear as a ceiling stain forty feet from the actual breach. This is why handyman patches so often fail: they seal the spot above the drip, not the hole in the roof. Our diagnostic sequence is built to beat that geometry. The drone survey maps the whole field first — ponding rings, coating wear, suspect seams, displaced flashings — so nothing is diagnosed through a keyhole. Moisture readings and core samples then confirm what is wet and what is dry, tracing the water back to its entry rather than its exit. On seamless foam roofs this lateral-travel problem barely exists, because the fully adhered foam gives water no path to wander — one of the quiet reasons owners who get tired of chronic leak-chasing eventually convert to SPF (the system is described in our spray polyurethane foam roofing guide).

Ponding Water: The Repair That Prevents the Next Five

Stand on almost any aging flat roof in Clovis a day after a winter storm and you will find the ponds — shallow lakes over deflected bays, around settled drains, behind equipment curbs. Ponding is not cosmetic. Standing water accelerates whatever weakness sits beneath it, multiplies the load on the structure, breeds the algae and debris that clog drains further, and in a hard freeze — which the Valley floor does get on clear winter nights — it becomes an ice lens working at every crack. Most repair contractors can only treat the symptom, because fixing ponding on a membrane roof means re-engineering slope. Foam gives us a better tool: because SPF is applied as an expanding liquid, we build up low areas and shape crickets around drains and curbs, restoring positive drainage as part of the repair itself. It is the single highest-leverage flat roof repair in this climate, because it removes the condition that was busy manufacturing your next three leaks.

Repair or Replace? A Straight Answer in 48 Hours

Every owner eventually faces the fork: keep repairing, or invest in a new system. We put the decision on paper with evidence. As a rule of thumb, a roof with localized damage, dry insulation, and a sound deck should be repaired; a roof with widespread saturation, repeated failures across the field, or a deck problem should be restored or replaced, because repairs on a failing field are rent, not equity.

Heat, Fog, and Why Clovis Roofs Crack on Schedule

It helps to understand the machine that is breaking your roof, because it runs on a calendar. Summer in Clovis delivers weeks above 100°F; a dark roof surface hits 160°F by afternoon and sheds forty-plus degrees overnight, and that daily expansion-contraction cycle works every seam, fastener, and flashing like a wire being bent back and forth. Autumn adds harvest dust from the ag country east toward Sanger — fine sediment that dams scuppers and holds moisture against the membrane. Then winter inverts the whole system: tule fog seals the Valley floor under a damp lid for days at a time, so the moisture summer let in never dries out, and the season's ten-to-twelve inches of rain arrive concentrated in exactly the months the roof is weakest. The practical takeaway is timing: the cheapest repair you will ever buy is the one done in September and October, after the heat has done its damage and before the storm season audits it. A fall drone survey costs you nothing and regularly saves Clovis owners the January emergency call.

Elevation adds a wrinkle on the east side of town. As Clovis climbs toward the Sierra foothills along the 168 corridor, buildings pick up more wind exposure than the sheltered valley floor, and the occasional cold snap bites harder — clear-night frosts that turn yesterday's pond into this morning's ice lens. Roofs out toward the foothill margins earn their fall inspection twice over, and anything mechanically fastened or loosely flashed up there should be checked after every significant wind event, not just annually. Meanwhile the older, lower parts of town fight the opposite micro-battle: mature street trees that fill scuppers with leaf-drop precisely when the first storms arrive. Same city, two different maintenance calendars — and one more reason a repair contractor should know the local map, not just the trade.

Common Questions

How much does flat roof repair cost in Clovis?add

It depends entirely on what failed, which is why we do not quote from the phone. A targeted repair — a split seam, a failed flashing, a puncture — is the cheapest work we do. If damage is widespread, restoration by silicone coating runs $2 to $4 per square foot and a foam overlay $4 to $8. The free drone assessment produces a written, itemized price within 48 hours.

My ceiling is leaking but I can't find the hole in the roof. Is that normal?add

Completely normal on flat roofs — water enters at one point and travels laterally along the deck before dripping, so the stain and the breach can be forty feet apart. That is why we survey the whole roof by drone and confirm with moisture readings and core samples instead of patching over the drip and hoping.

Can you repair a spray foam roof another company installed?add

Yes. Foam repair is our core trade: we cut out damaged foam, dry the substrate, fill with new foam, and re-coat with compatible silicone. We also undo a lot of incorrect repairs — asphalt mastic and mismatched coatings damage SPF. If the roof's coating is simply at the end of its 10-to-20-year cycle, a full recoat restores the whole system.

Do you offer emergency roof repair in Clovis?add

We prioritize active leaks and respond as fast as the schedule and weather honestly allow — our crews are about 55 minutes out in Tulare County, and we will give you a truthful arrival window on the phone rather than an invented guarantee. Should I repair my old flat roof again or replace it?add

The core samples decide. Localized damage over dry insulation is worth repairing. A structurally sound roof with a weathered surface is usually best restored — silicone coating or foam overlay — at a fraction of replacement cost. Widespread saturated insulation means repairs are throwing good money after bad, and we will show you the cores and say so.

What causes most roof leaks in Clovis?add

The climate one-two punch: 100-degree summer heat cycling splits seams and flashings, then the November-through-March storm season and weeks of tule fog exploit every opening while the roof stays too damp to dry out. Clogged drains from fall debris and ag dust are the most preventable culprit — half the emergency calls we run trace back to drainage.
