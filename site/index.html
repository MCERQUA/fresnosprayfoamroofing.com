Spray Foam Roofing Questions

Straight answers for commercial building owners.

How long does a spray foam roof last?add

With a maintained coating, SPF roofs perform for decades. The silicone topcoat is recoated every 10–20 years, which renews the entire system without tear-off — foam roofs over 30 years old routinely test like new underneath. Full data in our lifespan guide.

How much does spray foam roofing cost?add

New commercial SPF systems typically run $4–$8 per square foot installed; recoating an existing foam roof typically runs $2–$4. See the full 2026 cost guide — and get a firm written bid within 48 hours of your free assessment.

Can a foam roof be repaired instead of replaced?add

In most cases, yes — blisters, cracks, ponding, and worn coating are repaired or recoated at a fraction of replacement cost. See foam roof repair in Fresno.

Can spray foam go over my existing roof?add

Usually yes. SPF installs directly over most existing metal, built-up, and concrete roofs with no tear-off — saving removal and disposal costs while your building stays open.

Does a foam roof really cut cooling costs?add

Yes. SPF has the highest R-value per inch of any roofing material (about R-6.5 per inch), and the white silicone topcoat reflects most solar energy — a major advantage in Fresno's 100-degree summers.

How long does spray foam roofing take to install?add

Most commercial installations finish in under a week: about a day of prep, 1–3 days of foam application, and 1–2 days of coating — with your building open for business the whole time, since everything happens from the roof side. See the full installation walkthrough.

Is spray foam roofing better than TPO?add

For hot-climate flat roofs like the Central Valley, usually yes — SPF has zero seams, built-in insulation at ~R-6.5 per inch, and renews with a recoat instead of a tear-off. TPO's advantage is a lower first bid on simple new-construction decks. Full breakdown: spray foam vs TPO.

Does spray foam work on metal building roofs?add

It's one of the best uses of the system. Foam seals every seam and fastener head — the places metal roofs leak — and adds continuous insulation in the same application, with no tear-off.

Can you fix ponding water on a flat roof?add

Yes — SPF is the only roofing material where slope can be built into the application itself. We add foam to low spots to re-slope the roof and eliminate ponding permanently. Details on the foam roof repair page.

Fresno, Clovis, Visalia, Tulare, Hanford, Madera, Merced, and Bakersfield — all of the Central Valley from Merced County to Kern County, dispatched from our Tulare County shop.

Yes — the drone survey, physical inspection, core sampling where needed, and the written bid within 48 hours are all free, with no obligation. You get a real evaluation of repair, recoat, and new-system options priced side by side.
