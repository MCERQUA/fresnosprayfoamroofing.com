## What the data says

**Fresno-local commercial terms have almost no search volume.** "commercial roofing fresno" = 10/mo; "spray foam roofing fresno", "roof coating fresno", "flat roof repair fresno" etc. = 0/no data. A commercial-only exact-match domain cannot live on local query volume.

**SERP reality (Fresno geo, "foam roofing"):** #7 foamroofingfresno.com (EMD local), **#8 allstatesprayfoamroofing.com (the brand's own existing live site)**, #9 trustallroofing.com, then directories. The family already ranks; this site is the commercial-focus wedge.

**"commercial roofing fresno" top-10 is soft** (CentiMark/Nations Roof national pages, Yelp/BBB, one SaaS spam page) — a real local commercial page can reach top-5 even at 10/mo volume; the buyers behind that term are exactly the target customer.

**Brand collision:** allstateroofingcompany.com (unrelated Fresno "Allstate Roofing") ranks for commercial roofing fresno and owns "allstate roofing fresno" (70/mo, $78 CPC). The site must consistently say "Allstate **Spray Foam** Roofing" and disambiguate.

## Strategy

Position this domain as the **commercial/SPF-specialist** of the brand family (main site = insulation-led, allstatesprayfoamroofing.com = general foam roofing). Target the LOW-competition foam cluster with dedicated pages that rank nationally-localized, and own the soft "commercial roofing fresno" SERP with a real page.
